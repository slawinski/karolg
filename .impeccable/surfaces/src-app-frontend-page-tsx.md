# Surface brief — home (`/`)

Mode: Experience. The work leads from the first viewport; interface recedes.

## Audience, job, action

Casting directors and agencies (book), collaborators (explore stories), followers
(follow the person). One action everywhere: reach out — email or Instagram.
No duplicate CTA intents; one label per intent.

## Proof and content

Stories as editions (ED. 01–04): keeper print + variant pulls + selects.
Contact is the final pull. Demonstration imagery: the five existing photo crops,
procedurally treated as proof pulls (duotone separation, dot screen, multiply
overlaps). Replacement list for real material: commissioned portraits and
movement frames to slot into the same rack.

## Chosen direction and memorable moment

THE PROOF WALL (DESIGN.md). First viewport: poster-scale off-register chase
type with one proof drying beside it, the wall continuing below the fold.
Signature: squeegee-wipe reveals; hover misregisters the inks.

## Constraints

Brutalist-editorial family is binding. 5-tier responsive system must survive.
CMS data layer (`src/lib/portfolio.ts`, Payload collections) untouched.
Reduced-motion, 44px targets, AA contrast preserved.

## Unresolved

Real photography arrival date; whether Instagram or email becomes the single
primary CTA once booking flow is known.
